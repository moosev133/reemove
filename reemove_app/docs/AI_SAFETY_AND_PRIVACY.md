# AI Safety and Privacy Policy

## General principles

- AI output is assistance, not professional medical, nutritional, legal, or emergency advice.
- Minimize personal data sent to the model.
- Use age-group labels instead of exact birth dates.
- Do not send private messages, precise home addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses to the model.
- Store only the fields needed for product functionality and debugging.
- Provide deletion controls for user AI history.

## Workout safety

Generated plans must:

- Match the declared sport and experience level.
- Use moderate progression.
- Include warm-up, recovery, and stop-if-pain guidance.
- Avoid one-repetition-max tests, dangerous stunts, extreme training volume, punishment workouts, and training through pain.
- Refer users with injury symptoms to an appropriate qualified professional.

## Nutrition safety

Generated guidance must:

- Focus on balanced meals, hydration, regular eating, recovery, and practical food examples.
- Avoid body shaming, “ideal body” claims, starvation, fasting, dehydration, purging, diet pills, supplement dosing, and rapid weight change plans.
- Avoid calorie or macro prescriptions for users under 18.
- Encourage a parent/guardian and qualified professional for minors with medical or performance nutrition needs.

## Challenge safety

Generated challenges must:

- Be low or moderate difficulty.
- Be age-appropriate.
- Avoid traffic, rooftops, water-risk activities without supervision, extreme weather exposure, sleep deprivation, pain tolerance, breath-holding competitions, dangerous lifting, and illegal activity.
- Include a verification method that does not require risky behavior.

## Matchmaker safety

- Rank only real candidate IDs loaded server-side.
- Do not expose precise locations.
- Return approximate distance only.
- Apply existing block, privacy, age, and visibility filters before AI ranking.
- For minors, preserve the existing ReeMove age-safety and guardian controls.

## Content assistant safety

- Moderate the user’s source text before generation.
- Moderate generated captions before display.
- Do not generate harassment, sexual content involving minors, dangerous challenge promotion, or private identifying information.

## Human review

The following actions always require explicit human confirmation:

- Publishing a challenge.
- Publishing a caption or post.
- Sending a message to another user.
- Changing a trainer’s public business profile.
- Acting on a health or injury recommendation.
